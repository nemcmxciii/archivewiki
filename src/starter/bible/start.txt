===== About this edition =====

World English Bible Updated (WEB Updated), 2024 edition. The source archive identifies this edition as public domain and includes the Deuterocanonical books and sections. Esther (Greek edition) and Daniel (Greek edition) are complete Greek-text editions that include their Deuterocanonical material; the standard WEB Esther and Daniel pages remain available. Scripture text is kept separate from personal notes so it remains readable and unchanged.

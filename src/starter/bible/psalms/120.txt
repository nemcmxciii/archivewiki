====== Psalms 120 ======

[[bible:start|WEB Bible]] · [[bible:psalms:start|Psalms]]

----

''A Song of Ascents.''
**1** In my distress, I cried to the LORD.
He answered me.
**2** Deliver my soul, LORD, from lying lips,
from a deceitful tongue.
**3** What will be given to you, and what will be done more to you,
you deceitful tongue?
**4** Sharp arrows of the mighty,
with coals of juniper.
**5** Woe is me, that I live in Meshech,
that I dwell among the tents of Kedar!
**6** My soul has had her dwelling too long
with him who hates peace.
**7** I am for peace,
but when I speak, they are for war.

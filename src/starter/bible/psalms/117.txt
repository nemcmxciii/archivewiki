====== Psalms 117 ======

[[bible:start|WEB Bible]] · [[bible:psalms:start|Psalms]]

----

**1** Praise the LORD,((117:1 LORD or GOD in all caps is from the Hebrew יהוה Yahweh except when otherwise noted as being from the short form יה Yah.)) all you nations!
Extol him, all you peoples!
**2** For his loving kindness is great toward us.
The LORD’s faithfulness endures forever.
Praise the LORD!((117:2 Hebrew: יה Yah))

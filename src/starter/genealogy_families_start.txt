====== Family Groups ======

Use a family group sheet to keep the details of a family unit together. Enter the names you know; dates, sources, and other details can be added later.

===== Create a family group =====

Search for and select an existing person to connect their page to this sheet. Their details will be filled in for you; changes you make update their person page. Choose **Clear person selection** to start a new person instead. New names get their own person pages. Choose relationship types for partners and children, and decide whether each parent or partner should appear as a father, mother, or family-only connection in ancestor charts.

===== Your family groups =====

Saved sheets appear here. Open one to review or update it; its people are linked to their individual pages, ancestor charts, and Descendants view. Changing a relationship type does not automatically remove older parent links from person pages.

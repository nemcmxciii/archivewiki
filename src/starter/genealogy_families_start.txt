====== Family Groups ======

Use a family group sheet to keep the details of a household together. Enter the names you know; dates, sources, and other details can be added later.

===== Create a family group =====

If a name matches an existing person page, the sheet connects to it. A new name gets its own person page.

{{familygroup>new}}

===== Your family groups =====

Saved sheets appear here. Open one to review or update it; its people are linked to their individual pages and family trees.

{{families>}}

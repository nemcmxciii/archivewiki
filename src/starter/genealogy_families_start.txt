====== Family Groups ======

Use a family group sheet to keep the details of a household together. Enter the names you know; dates, sources, and other details can be added later.

===== Create a family group =====

Search for and select an existing person to connect their page to this sheet. Their details will be filled in for you; changes you make update their person page. Choose **Clear person selection** to start a new person instead. New names get their own person pages.

{{familygroup>new}}

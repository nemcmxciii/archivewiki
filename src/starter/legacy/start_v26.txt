====== ArchiveWiki ======

Your private archive is stored on this Mac. Keep notes, family research, records, and references in one place.

===== Getting started =====

  * **Write a note:** Choose **File → New Note**. ArchiveWiki starts a dated entry in your Journal.
  * **Create a page:** Enter its title in **Find or add a page** below. No special formatting is needed for a basic page.
  * **Organize by topic:** Open Journal, Genealogy, Portfolio, or Reference below and follow the instructions there.
  * **Find it later:** Choose **View → Search Archive** (⌘K), or open a page under **Recent pages** below.

===== Browse your archive =====

  * [[journal:start|Journal]] — dated entries and notes
  * [[genealogy:start|Genealogy]] — family and research subjects
  * [[portfolio:start|Portfolio]] — residential, employment and credentials history
  * [[reference:start|Reference]] — sources, links and documents

===== Recent pages =====

Recently opened pages appear here. This list is stored only on this Mac.

===== Find or add a page =====

Use **View → Search Archive** (⌘K) to find existing pages. For a page in a specific section, create a link to it (for example ''%%[[genealogy:jane_doe]]%%''), then click the link and choose **Create this page**.

===== Back up your archive =====

Use **File → Back Up & Restore → Back Up Now** to save a copy before big changes. ArchiveWiki also makes daily backups automatically; turn this off or on in the same menu. Keep an extra copy somewhere separate from this Mac.
To keep separate collections of pages, choose **File → Manage Wikis**. Each wiki has its own pages and backups; switching reopens ArchiveWiki on the selected wiki.

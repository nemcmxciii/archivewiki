====== Genealogy ======

One page per person, for example ''%%[[genealogy:jane_doe]]%%''. A new page here starts from a template with a small details block (name, dates, places, parents) and a fillable five-generation form for that person's ancestors.

  * [[genealogy:chart|Five-generation chart]] — pick anyone and fill in or edit their ancestors
  * [[genealogy:chart_blank|Blank chart]] — print a chart to fill in by hand

**How it works:** the form lists the person, then their parents, grandparents, great-grandparents and 2nd great-grandparents, one generation after another. Type a name to unlock the next generation, fill in dates and places, tick **Living** where it applies, and press **Save chart**. Saving creates a page for each new person and links them as father or mother. The **Tree** link shows the traditional chart for viewing and printing.

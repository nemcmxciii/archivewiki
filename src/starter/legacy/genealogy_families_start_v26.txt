====== Family Groups ======

A Family Group sheet keeps the people in one household together. Enter the parent names, marriage or partnership details, children, and sources you have. A name matching an existing person page will connect to it; a new name creates a person page. You can begin with names and fill in the rest later.

===== Create a family group =====

{{familygroup>new}}

===== Your family groups =====

Open a saved sheet to review or update it. The people on each sheet are linked to their individual pages and to their Pedigree Trees.

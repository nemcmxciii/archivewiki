====== Journal ======

**Today's Journal Entry** (File → Today's Journal Entry, ⇧⌘J) opens today's dated page.

**New Note** (File → New Note, ⇧⌘N) captures a quick thought in a timestamped page. Rename or move it later if you like.

Entries are regular wiki pages. Use **View → Search Archive** (⌘K) to find them later.

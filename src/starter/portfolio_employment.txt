====== Employment history ======

Keep the details most often requested for work history. Forms vary, so only enter what you need.

===== Add an employment record =====

Use one entry for each job. Leave optional fields blank when they don't apply.

<WRAP aw-employment-form>
<form>
action template portfolio:employment:entry_template portfolio:employment:job_@@Record number@@
thanks "Employment record saved. Open the new record below to review or edit it."
hiddenautoinc "Record number" 0000
hidden "recordtype" =employment
textbox "Employer name"
textbox "Employer address or work location" !
textbox "Job title"
textbox "From (MM/YYYY)"
textbox "To (MM/YYYY; leave blank if current)" !
textarea "Main duties and accomplishments" x4 !
textbox "Supervisor or reference name (optional)" !
textbox "Reference phone or email (optional)" !
textbox "Hours or work schedule (optional)" !
textbox "Pay rate and pay period (optional)" !
textarea "Reason for leaving (optional)" x2 !
submit "Save employment record"
</form>
</WRAP>

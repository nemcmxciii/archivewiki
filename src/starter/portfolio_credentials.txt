====== Education & credentials ======

Keep degrees, licenses, certificates, and relevant training ready for applications. Forms vary, so only enter what you need.

===== Add a credential =====

Use one entry for each degree, license, certificate, or training program. Leave optional fields blank when they don't apply.

<WRAP aw-credential-form>
<form>
action template portfolio:credentials:entry_template portfolio:credentials:credential_@@Record number@@
thanks "Credential saved. Open the new record below to review or edit it."
hiddenautoinc "Record number" 0000
hidden "recordtype" =credential
select "Type" "Degree|License|Certification|Training|Other"
textbox "Credential or program name"
textbox "School or issuing organization"
textbox "Dates attended (MM/YYYY; optional)" !
textbox "Field or specialization (optional)" !
textbox "Date earned or completed (optional)" !
select "Status" "Completed|Active|Expired|In progress"
textbox "Expiration or renewal date (optional)" !
textbox "License or certificate number and state (optional)" !
textbox "Verification or document location (optional)" !
submit "Save credential"
</form>
</WRAP>

====== Family history ======

Start with what you know. Add a person's name now; dates, places, and the rest of the story can come later. Each person has a page of their own, and you can connect people as you learn more.

===== Add a person =====

Enter a name to create a person page. Dates and places are optional, so it is fine to leave them blank.

{{newperson>}}

===== Explore family connections =====

  * [[genealogy:families:start|Create a family group]] — record parent or partner roles, children, relationships, and sources on one sheet.
  * [[genealogy:chart|Build a family tree]] — follow parents and earlier generations, or switch to Descendants to follow children and family groups.
  * [[genealogy:chart_blank|Print a blank family tree]] — fill it in by hand.

===== Find a person =====

Search by name, date, or place. Open a person's page to see their details, family connections, sources, and notes.

{{people>}}

===== Helpful to know =====

  * You can add details or correct them later. Nothing needs to be complete before you begin.
  * Add sources to remember where a detail came from and to help you find the original record again.
  * The ancestor chart focuses on parents; the Descendants view follows children and family groups. Living relatives are marked in the tree, and the redacted view hides their names.

Your family pages and research are stored in this wiki on this Mac.

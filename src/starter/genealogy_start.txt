====== Family archive ======

Keep one page for each person, then connect the people you know. You can start with a name and fill in the rest as you find it.

===== Add a person =====

Enter a name to create a person page. Dates and places are optional; you can add or change them later.

{{newperson>}}

===== Explore your family =====

  * [[genealogy:chart|Build or update a family tree]] — choose a person and add known ancestors one generation at a time.
  * [[genealogy:chart_blank|Print a blank family chart]] — fill it in by hand.

===== Find a person =====

Search by name, date, or place. Open a person's page to read their details, family links, sources, and research notes.

{{people>}}

===== How it works =====

  * Each person has their own page. Begin with only a name; details can be added whenever you are ready.
  * The ancestor chart guides you through parents, grandparents, and earlier generations. You can save partial work and return later.
  * Record sources on each person’s page so you can return to the records behind your family history.
  * The tree view is useful for seeing or printing a family at a glance. Living people are marked in the chart; the redacted tree view can hide their names.

Your family pages and research stay in this local archive.

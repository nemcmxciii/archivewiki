====== Residential history ======

Keep the address details most often requested for housing applications. Forms vary, so only enter what you need.

===== Add a residence =====

Use one entry for each address. Leave optional fields blank when they don't apply.

<WRAP aw-residence-form>
<form>
action template portfolio:residential:entry_template portfolio:residential:residence_@@Record number@@
thanks "Residence saved. Open the new record below to review or edit it."
hiddenautoinc "Record number" 0000
hidden "recordtype" =residence
textbox "Street address"
textbox "Unit or apartment" !
textbox "City, state, ZIP"
select "Housing type" "Rent|Own|Other"
textbox "From (MM/YYYY)"
textbox "To (MM/YYYY; leave blank if current)" !
textbox "Monthly rent or mortgage (optional)" !
textbox "Landlord or property manager (optional)" !
textbox "Landlord phone or email (optional)" !
textarea "Reason for moving (optional)" x3 !
submit "Save residence"
</form>
</WRAP>

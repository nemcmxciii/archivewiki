====== Pedigree Trees ======

Choose the person whose ancestors you want to record. The form shows one generation at a time. Search for and select an existing relative to link their page, or enter a new name to create a person page. Linking an existing person does not change their details. Add relatives as you learn about them, move forward when you are ready, and choose **Save changes** whenever you want to keep your progress. You can leave details blank and return later.

Use **Tree** to view or print the pedigree. Start from the person you want in the center; this chart follows parents and earlier ancestors.

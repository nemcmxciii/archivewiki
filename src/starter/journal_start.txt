====== Journal ======

Use your Journal for thoughts, plans, and moments you want to remember. A quick note or an unfinished entry is enough; you can come back to it later.

If you prefer keyboard shortcuts, use **⇧⌘J** for today's entry or **⇧⌘N** for a quick note. To find something later, choose **View → Search Archive** (⌘K).
